3 · Your 30-day plan — spreadsheet templates on Etsy

Copied from Emily McDermott's playbook ($250k in <2 yrs), aimed at a UK gap.

What 30 days gets you: a live, well-optimised shop with ~20–30 listings and your first few sales (£0–150). Not thousands — the asset compounds over 6–12 months.

Your niche (your edge — most competitors are American):

  • Start: finance trackers for side-hustlers/resellers (Vinted/eBay reseller profit tracker, Etsy seller bookkeeping, pricing calculator). You are this buyer; competition is thin.
  • Expand (month 2): UK sole-trader / Self-Assessment bookkeeping — a real gap, with Making Tax Digital rolling out April 2026→2028. List it as "tax-prep / record keeping", not "MTD compliant".
  • Skip for now: generic personal/family budgets (most saturated).

Tools & cost to launch ≈ £5: Google Sheets (£0), Canva free (£0), eRank free (£0), Etsy listing fees ~£0.16 × ~25 = ~£4. You keep ~85% per sale (on a £10 sale Etsy takes ~£1.55, no cost of goods).

Delivery mechanic (protects your master):

  1. Build in Google Sheets; Share → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer only.
  2. Change the URL ending to /template/preview so buyers make their own copy.
  3. Canva 1-page PDF with that link + instructions → upload the PDF to Etsy (never the live sheet).

Keywords (free): Etsy autocomplete → eRank → longtail phrases under ~2,000 searches → main keyword at the start of the title → all 13 tags different → separate listing for the Excel version (that's how you reach 25 listings from ~8 builds).

Week by week:

  • Week 1: open shop, set up eRank/Canva, keyword research, build template #1 end-to-end.
  • Week 2: build 4–5 more templates, make images + 15-sec demos, publish 8–10 listings.
  • Week 3: build 2–3 bundles, reach ~20–25 listings, add photo/bio, get a first review.
  • Week 4: check stats (views vs sales), start a free email list with a freebie, refresh weak listings, plan the UK tax range.
🇬🇧 UK tax: first £1,000/yr is covered by the trading allowance (tax-free, no registration); above that, register for Self-Assessment. Etsy reports sellers to HMRC — declare it. (General guidance, not tax advice.)
