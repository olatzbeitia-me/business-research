# 30-Day Launch Plan — Selling Spreadsheet Templates on Etsy

*Reverse-engineered from Emily McDermott (Pretty Arrow), a top-0.1% Etsy seller who did **$250k in under 2 years** selling budget spreadsheets. This is her actual playbook, adapted to your situation with a UK edge she doesn't have. Source: [Side Hustle Nation](https://www.sidehustlenation.com/selling-spreadsheets-on-etsy/).*

---

## Set expectations first (the honest bit)

**What 30 days gets you:** a live, properly-optimised Etsy shop with ~20–30 listings and your first handful of sales (realistically £0–£150). **It does not get you thousands.** Emily took ~2 years to reach $250k; her first spreadsheet sold *2 copies* on day one. Etsy SEO takes weeks to index and months to mature. The 30 days is about **building the asset correctly** so it compounds — not about month-one income.

**Why this model over the others:** ~85% net margin (pure digital, no supplier cut, no shipping), you make it once and sell it forever, and it runs entirely around school hours. The catch is saturation — which is exactly why the *niche* choice below matters more than anything else.

## The playbook you're copying (Emily's, distilled)

| Lever | What she did |
|-------|--------------|
| **Niche** | Found an *underserved audience* (young women + personal finance) whose existing options were ugly ("all greens and blues"). Differentiated on colour + charts. |
| **Products** | Debt-payoff calculators, monthly budgets, budget-by-paycheck. £4–£20 each, premiums up to ~£55, **bundles drove the six-figure growth**. |
| **SEO** | Free tool **eRank**. Targeted **longtail keywords under ~2,000 searches**. Main keyword at the *start* of the title. Avoided competitive terms like "budget planner". |
| **Listings** | Separate listing for each keyword variation (e.g. "Google Sheets" vs "Excel"). All image slots used. 15-second demo video. Personal photo + bio for trust. |
| **Delivery** | A Canva PDF containing a "make a copy" link to the Google Sheet (never the live sheet itself). |
| **Time** | ~1–2 hrs/day, 4 days/week. |

**Her one-line lesson:** *"Success came from understanding the customer, not spreadsheet expertise."* You don't need to be an Excel wizard. You need to pick the right buyer.

## Your niche — and your unfair advantage

Emily's exact niche (personal budgeting) is now crowded *because* she proved it. Don't copy the niche — copy the *method*, pointed at a gap. Your edge: **you're a UK-based side-hustler researching side hustles.** Two things fall out of that, and almost every competitor on Etsy is American and can't touch them:

### 🎯 Recommended wedge: **finance trackers for side-hustlers & resellers**
You *are* this buyer. You understand their pain, and this audience is booming (and has money). Products:
- **Vinted/Depop/eBay reseller profit & inventory tracker** — sourcing cost, sale price, fees, postage, profit-per-item. (Ties directly to the Jess Morton reselling model from the last report — you can sell to that whole audience.)
- **Etsy seller bookkeeping spreadsheet** — a proven bestseller category.
- **Print-on-demand / small-shop profit calculator.**

This is *less* saturated than personal budgets, and "pretty planner" sellers don't compete here.

### 🎯 Expansion (month 2+): **UK sole-trader / Self-Assessment bookkeeping**
Almost every bookkeeping template on Etsy uses US tax categories. A clean **income & expense tracker built around HMRC Self-Assessment categories** is a genuine UK gap. And there's a real tailwind: **Making Tax Digital for Income Tax** starts April 2026 (£50k+ sole traders/landlords), April 2027 (£30k+), April 2028 (£20k+) — pushing hundreds of thousands of sole traders off paper and toward digital records. ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax))
- *Honest caveat:* a spreadsheet is a **record-keeping on-ramp**, not full MTD compliance (final submission needs MTD-compatible software). List it as "self-assessment record keeping / tax-prep," not "MTD compliant," and you're both accurate and useful.

**Skip for now:** generic personal/family budgets — highest saturation, hardest to rank cold.

## The delivery mechanic (do this once, reuse forever)

Etsy won't host a live Google link, so:
1. Build the template in Google Sheets. **Lock it down:** File → Share → "Anyone with the link" → **Viewer** (never Editor).
2. Take the sheet's URL and **replace `/edit...` at the end with `/template/preview`** — this forces buyers to *make their own copy* and protects your master.
3. In Canva, make a 1-page branded PDF: "Thanks! Click here to get your template →" with that link, plus 3 lines of setup instructions and a support email.
4. Upload **the PDF** as the Etsy digital file. (Optionally include an Excel `.xlsx` version too — sell it as a *separate listing* to capture "Excel template" searches.)

## Pricing & bundles

- **Single templates:** £4–£12 to start (lower gets you first reviews faster).
- **Premium/comprehensive:** £15–£30.
- **Bundles** (this is where the money is): combine 3–4 related sheets at a "£X off" price. e.g. *"Side-Hustle Finance Pack"* = reseller tracker + pricing calculator + mileage log for £24 instead of £36.
- Anchor every listing with a bundle upsell in the description and images.

## The keyword method (free, 10 min per listing)

1. Type your seed into the **Etsy search bar** — the autocomplete *is* real buyer demand. Note every suggestion.
2. Put those into **eRank** → keep **longtail phrases with searches under ~2,000 and lower competition** (eRank's "beginner-friendly" filter). These are winnable cold.
3. **Title:** lead with the main keyword — *"Reseller Profit Tracker Google Sheets, Vinted eBay Inventory & Sales Spreadsheet, Small Business Bookkeeping"*.
4. **Tags:** use all 13, each a *different* longtail phrase (don't repeat title words uselessly). Include "Google Sheets", "Excel template", "digital download".
5. **Make a separate listing** for the Excel version and for each distinct keyword angle — that's how you legitimately reach 25+ listings from ~8 real builds.

## The 30-day plan

**Week 1 — Foundation & first build**
- Open the Etsy shop (name it for the niche, e.g. something like "…LedgerCo" / "…SheetStudio"). Set up payments + policies for digital goods (instant download, no refunds on digital).
- Sign up for free eRank + Canva.
- Keyword-research the reseller/side-hustle niche; pick your first 5 target keywords.
- **Build template #1** (the Vinted/reseller profit tracker) end-to-end, including the delivery PDF. Get the *whole pipeline* working once.

**Week 2 — Get to a shelf-worth of listings**
- Build 4–5 more core templates (Etsy bookkeeping, pricing calculator, mileage log, variable-income budget).
- Create listing images in Canva (use all image slots; add a 15-sec screen-recording demo — hugely improves conversion).
- Publish your first **8–10 listings** (core products + their Excel/keyword variations).

**Week 3 — Volume, bundles & trust**
- Build 2–3 **bundles** and list them.
- Add keyword-variation listings to reach **~20–25 live listings**.
- Add a personal photo + short bio to your shop ("UK mum, built these running my own side hustle") — trust converts.
- Ask any early buyer (or a friend who'll genuinely use one) for a first review.

**Week 4 — Optimise & seed repeat traffic**
- Check eRank/Etsy stats: which listings get *views* but no sales (fix images/price) vs no views (fix keywords).
- Add a free "starter" sheet as a lead magnet and start a simple email list (free ConvertKit/MailerLite tier) — Emily's list of 2,000+ was a major growth driver.
- Refresh titles/tags on the weakest listings. Plan month 2's UK sole-trader/self-assessment range.

**Ongoing rhythm after day 30:** add ~1–2 listings/week, refresh SEO monthly, expand bundles. Consistency is the entire game.
