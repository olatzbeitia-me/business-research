# business-research

An automated intelligence bot that surfaces easy, profitable global business models I can realistically replicate in the UK — built for a working mum who needs flexible, low-capital side hustles.

## What it does

Runs three times a week and scans globally for emerging, trending, or underreported business models. For each one it produces:

- **What it is** — plain-English explanation of how it works and makes money
- **Why it's profitable** — margins, pricing, demand drivers, moat
- **Working-mum fit score** — flexibility, startup effort, time-per-week (scored /10)
- **Replication roadmap** — the minimum viable version, skills/resources, realistic costs
- **Verdict** — Strong opportunity · Worth watching · Not suitable, with the reason why

### Filtering rules (a model must pass all three)
1. Can be run as a **side hustle**, not a full-time operation from day one
2. **Proven profitable somewhere right now** — not theoretical
3. **No constant availability or in-person presence** that clashes with family life

### Market updates
Featured models are tracked in [`WATCHLIST.md`](./WATCHLIST.md). When one shows a significant shift — new competition, regulation, a viral spike, or a notable founder outcome — it's flagged in the next report as **[MARKET UPDATE]**.
